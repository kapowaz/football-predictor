List of Crewe Alexandra F.C. seasons

[Crewe Alexandra Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crewe_Alexandra_Football_Club) is a professional [association football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) club based in the town of [Crewe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crewe), [Cheshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheshire), England. Nicknamed The Railwaymen because of the town's [links with the rail industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crewe_Works), they play at [Gresty Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gresty_Road). The team compete in [League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_League_Two), the fourth tier of the [English football league system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_football_league_system).

## Key

Key to league record

- Top scorer and number of goals scored shown in bold when he was also top scorer for the division.
- Position = Position in the final league table
- Pts = Points
- GD = Goals difference
- GA = Goals against
- GF = Goals for
- L = Games lost
- D = Games drawn
- W = Games won
- Pld = Games played
- Level = Level of the league in the current league system

Key to cup records

- W = Winners
- A (QF, SF, F) = Area quarter-, semi-, finalists
- F = Finalists
- SF = Semi-finalists
- QF = Quarter-finalists
- R1 (2, etc.) = Proper Cup rounds
- G = Group stage
- QR1 (2, etc.) = Qualifying Cup rounds
- PR = Preliminary round
- Rec = Final club record in the form of wins-draws-losses
- Res = Final reached round
