List of Colchester United F.C. seasons

[Colchester United Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colchester_United_F.C.) is an English professional [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) club based in [Colchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colchester), [Essex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essex), that was founded in 1937.

Colchester United competed in the [Southern Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Football_League) from 1937 until 1950, when they were elected to the [Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Football_League). During this time, Colchester produced one of the most notable [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup) runs by a non-league side in [1947–48](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1947%E2%80%9348_FA_Cup), as they defeated fellow non-leaguers [Banbury Spencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banbury_Spencer_F.C.) in the first round, before beating Football League clubs [Wrexham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrexham_A.F.C.), [Huddersfield Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huddersfield_Town_A.F.C.) and [Bradford Park Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradford_Park_Avenue_A.F.C.). They finally fell to [Blackpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackpool_F.C.) in the fifth round.

Colchester played in the [Third Division South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Third_Division_South) for eight seasons, until the league was re-organised at the end of the [1957–58](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1957%E2%80%9358_Football_League) season. They finished in 12th position in the table that year, meaning that from the [1958–59](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1958%E2%80%9359_Football_League) season, the U's would play in the [Third Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Third_Division). Colchester remained in the Third Division until they were relegated in [1961](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1960%E2%80%9361_Football_League), but made an immediate return to the third tier when they finished the [1961–62](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1961%E2%80%9362_Football_League) season in second position, one point behind champions [Millwall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millwall_F.C.).

Three years later, the club finished 23rd of 24 clubs in the Third Division, as they were relegated back to the [Fourth Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Fourth_Division). Another single season in the fourth tier followed as Colchester were promoted in fourth position. Their spell in the Third Division was brief, as they were once again relegated in [1968](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1967%E2%80%9368_Football_League).

The U's remained in the Fourth Division for a further six seasons, but during this period, they embarked on one of the most notable runs in FA Cup history. Manager [Dick Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dick_Graham) took his ageing side to the [1970–71](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1970%E2%80%9371_FA_Cup) quarter-finals. They dispatched non-league side [Ringmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ringmer_F.C.) in the first round, before knocking-out [Cambridge United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_United_F.C.), [Barnet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnet_F.C.) and [Rochdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rochdale_A.F.C.) following a replay. United faced [Don Revie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Revie)'s formidable [Leeds United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeds_United_A.F.C.) side in the fifth round, who were at the top of the [First Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_First_Division) at the time. The U's raced to an unprecedented 3–0 lead in the match, before Leeds pulled two goals back. The match ended 3–2 to Colchester to record a famous giant-killing victory. The U's then faced [Everton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everton_F.C.) in the quarter-final but lost 5–0 at [Goodison Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodison_Park). Three seasons later, Colchester sealed promotion again as they ended the [1973–74](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1973%E2%80%9374_Football_League) season in third place.

Relegation from the Third Division followed in [1976](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1975%E2%80%9376_Football_League), with promotion again following one season later. Colchester returned to the Fourth Division for the final time in [1981](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1980%E2%80%9381_Football_League) as they finished 22nd of 24 teams. Colchester struggled financially in the late 1980s and suffered a significant drop in form, resulting in the club finishing bottom of the entire Football League in [1990](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989%E2%80%9390_Football_League) and as such were relegated to the [Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_League_(division)).

Colchester returned to the Football League at the second attempt, finishing second to Barnet in [1990–91](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990%E2%80%9391_Football_Conference), before beating [Wycombe Wanderers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wycombe_Wanderers_F.C.) to the title in [1991–92](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991%E2%80%9392_Football_Conference). The club completed a league and cup double as they lifted the [FA Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Trophy). Now in the recently renamed Division Three, Colchester earned a [play-off](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Football_League_play-offs) spot in the [1995–96](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995%E2%80%9396_Football_League) season, but lost in the semi-finals to [Plymouth Argyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plymouth_Argyle_F.C.). The U's reached [Wembley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wembley_Stadium_(1923)) for the first time for the [1997 Football League Trophy Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997_Football_League_Trophy_Final), but were defeated on [penalties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_shoot-out_(association_football)) by [Carlisle United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlisle_United_F.C.).

Colchester did achieve Wembley success the following season as they beat [Torquay United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torquay_United_F.C.) 1–0 in the [1998 Football League Third Division play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_Football_League_Third_Division_play-off_final). The club consolidated their position in the third tier for a number of seasons, before they achieved another feat in earning promotion to the [Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Championship) in [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005%E2%80%9306_Football_League) by finishing in second position to [Southend United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southend_United_F.C.). Colchester then finished in 10th position in the Championship, their highest-ever league finish. However, the following season, they were relegated back to [League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_League_One).

Following relegation from the Championship, Colchester remained in League One for eight seasons. Initially the club fought for the play-off positions, but began to struggle in the lower reaches of the league by the [2012–13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012%E2%80%9313_Football_League_One) season, narrowly avoiding relegation that year. Colchester were relegated from League One to the fourth tier of English football for the first time in 18 years at the end of the [2015–16](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015%E2%80%9316_Football_League_One) season.

In [League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_League_Two), Colchester were competitive, finishing in the top-half of the table regularly. The club reached the [2019–20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019-20_EFL_League_Two) play-offs, but were eliminated in the semi-finals by [Exeter City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_City_F.C.). The following season, Colchester struggled amidst the constraints of the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic), only securing their League Two status in the penultimate game of the season.

Since their formation in 1937, as of the [2021–22 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_Colchester_United_F.C._season), Colchester United have spent two seasons in the second tier of English football, 37 seasons in the third tier, 30 seasons in the fourth tier and two seasons in the Football Conference. Prior to their 1950 election to the Football League, Colchester spent seven seasons in the Southern League.
