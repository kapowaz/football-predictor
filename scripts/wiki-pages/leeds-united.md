List of Leeds United F.C. seasons

The statue of [Billy Bremner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Bremner), who played for [Leeds United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeds_United_F.C.) between 1959 and 1976 and captained them to cup success in [1968](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1968_Football_League_Cup_Final) and [1972](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_FA_Cup_Final).

[Leeds United Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeds_United_F.C.) were founded in October 1919, taking the place in the [Midland League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midland_Football_League_(1889)) vacated by Leeds City Reserves, and were elected to [The Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_League) for the [1920–21 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1920%E2%80%9321_in_English_football). They won the [Second Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Second_Division) title four years later to gain [promotion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_and_relegation) to the top tier of English football, but had to wait almost 50 years before winning any major [silverware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silverware_(sport)), with success in the [1968 Football League Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1968_Football_League_Cup_final) accompanied by success in Europe in the [final of the Inter-Cities Fairs Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1968_Inter-Cities_Fairs_Cup_final). The following season brought league success when Leeds won the [First Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_First_Division) championship. Their success continued into the 1970s with victory in the [1971 Inter-Cities Fairs Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1971_Inter-Cities_Fairs_Cup_final), the [1972 FA Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_FA_Cup_final), another league title in the [1973–74 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1973%E2%80%9374_in_English_football) and runners-up in the [1975 European Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1975_European_Cup_final). Leeds won the Second Division title in the [1989–90 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989-90_in_English_football) and were promoted back to the First Division. A third league title in the [1991–92 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991%E2%80%9392_in_English_football), a second FA Charity Shield in the [1992 FA Charity Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_FA_Charity_Shield) and a semi-final appearance in the [UEFA Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Champions_League) in [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000%E2%80%9301_UEFA_Champions_League), preceded a period of turbulence which culminated in relegation to [League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_One) in the [2006–07 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006%E2%80%9307_in_English_football). They dropped out of the top two tiers of English football for the first time in their history, in a season that saw them deducted ten points for going into [administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administration_(insolvency)). In 2010, Leeds United were promoted back into the [Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Championship). Ten years later, in 2020, the club were promoted back to the [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League) after a 16-year absence.

Leeds league performances

Leeds United have won the [League Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_English_football_champions) three times, four [Second Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Second_Division) titles, the [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup) once, the [League Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup) once, the [Charity Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Community_Shield) twice and the [Inter-Cities Fairs Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter-Cities_Fairs_Cup) twice. All these honours were won under the management of either [Arthur Fairclough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Fairclough_(manager)), [Don Revie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Revie), [Howard Wilkinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Wilkinson) and [Marcelo Bielsa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcelo_Bielsa). The club have also been runners-up five times in the League Championship, three times in the FA Cup, once each in the League Cup, the Charity Shield, the Inter-Cities Fairs Cup, the [Cup Winners' Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Cup_Winners'_Cup) and the [European Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Champions_League), and lost [the play-off](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter-Cities_Fairs_Cup_Trophy_Play-Off) to keep the Inter-City Fairs Cup trophy.

## Notes

1. Lost the [play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_EFL_Championship_play-off_final) to [Southampton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southampton_F.C.) 1–0 at the new [Wembley Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wembley_Stadium).
2. 35 of the 38 games of Leeds United's [2020–21 Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Premier_League) season were played behind closed doors due to the [COVID-19 pandemic in the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_the_United_Kingdom). The games played with fans also had strict restrictions on the number of fans allowed in attendance.
3. Lost the [play-off semi-final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_English_Football_League_play-offs#Championship_Semi-finals) to [Derby County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derby_County_F.C.) 4–3 on aggregate.
4. Lost the [play-off semi-final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_One_play-offs#2009) to [Millwall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millwall_F.C.) 2–1 on aggregate.
5. Lost the [play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_One_play-offs#2008) to [Doncaster Rovers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doncaster_Rovers_F.C.) 1–0 at the new [Wembley Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wembley_Stadium). Also lowest League finish.
6. Deducted 15 points for exiting [administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administration_(insolvency)) without going via a company voluntary agreement.
7. Deducted 10 points for entering [administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administration_(insolvency)).

[3–0](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Football_League_Championship_playoff_final) at the [Millennium Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millennium_Stadium).

10. 18 league goals.
