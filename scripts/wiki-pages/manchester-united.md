[Manchester United Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_United_F.C.) is an English professional [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) club based in [Old Trafford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Trafford_(area)), [Greater Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Manchester). The club was formed in [Newton Heath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newton_Heath) in 1878 as Newton Heath LYR F.C., and played their first competitive match in October 1886, when they entered the First Round of the [1886–87 FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1886%E2%80%9387_FA_Cup). The club was renamed Manchester United F.C. in 1902, and moved to [Old Trafford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Trafford) in 1910.

The club has won a total of 68 major trophies: the [League Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_English_football_champions) a joint-record twenty times (seven times in the [Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_First_Division) era and a record thirteen times in the [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League) era), the [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup) thirteen times, the [League Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup) six times, the [Community Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Community_Shield) a record 21 times (including four shared titles), the [European Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Champions_League) three times, the [UEFA Europa League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Europa_League) once, the [European Cup Winners' Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Cup_Winners'_Cup) once, the [European Super Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Super_Cup) once, the [Intercontinental Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intercontinental_Cup_(1960%E2%80%932004)) once and the [FIFA Club World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Club_World_Cup) once. The club has also never been out of the top two divisions of English football since entering the Football League. As of the end of the [2024–25 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Manchester_United_F.C._season), the club has played a total of 6,001 competitive matches, and has competed in the [top flight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_football_league_system) for 100 of their 126 seasons.

This list details the club's achievements in major competitions, and the top scorers for each season. Top scorers in bold were also the top scorers in the English league that season. Records of competitions such as the Lancashire Cup and the Manchester and District Challenge Cup are not included due to them being considered of less importance than the FA Cup and the League Cup.

## History

The club formed in 1878 as Newton Heath LYR F.C. At this time organised League football did not exist; "first class matches" were arranged on a largely ad-hoc basis and supplemented by [cup competitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single-elimination_tournament). Official records from these matches are sketchy at best, and are often extrapolated from newspaper reports at the time. In 1886, the club entered the [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup) for the first time, but were knocked out in the first round. They entered [The Combination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Combination) in 1888, but the league was wound up before the season could be completed. The club then joined the [Football Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_Alliance) in 1889, and in 1892 were elected to the newly formed [Football League First Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_First_Division). Upon joining the [Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Football_League), the club dropped the "LYR" from their name, before financial troubles forced the club to restructure in 1902, including a change of name to Manchester United F.C.

In [1956–57](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1956%E2%80%9357_Manchester_United_F.C._season), Manchester United became the first English club to enter European competition, entering the [European Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Champions_League), following [the Football Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_Association)'s refusal to allow [Chelsea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chelsea_F.C.) to enter the previous year. Eleven years later, in [1968](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1967%E2%80%9368_Manchester_United_F.C._season), they became the first English club to win the European Cup, and only the second British side after [Celtic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celtic_F.C.) had won it the year before. Meanwhile, in [1960–61](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1960%E2%80%9361_Manchester_United_F.C._season), Manchester United entered the inaugural [Football League Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup), only to decline to enter for the next five years. In [1992–93](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992%E2%80%9393_Manchester_United_F.C._season), they became founder members and inaugural champions of the [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League), and, in [1998–99](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998%E2%80%9399_Manchester_United_F.C._season), they won an unprecedented [Treble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treble_(association_football)) of the Premier League, FA Cup and UEFA Champions League. This was followed by two more Premier League titles in [1999–2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1999%E2%80%932000_Manchester_United_F.C._season) and [2000–01](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000%E2%80%9301_Manchester_United_F.C._season), making Manchester United only the fourth club to win three consecutive English league titles. The club picked up their 10th Premier League title in the [2007–08 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007%E2%80%9308_Manchester_United_F.C._season), and followed it with a third Champions League title 10 days later. The [following season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008%E2%80%9309_Manchester_United_F.C._season), Manchester United became the first British club to win the [FIFA Club World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Club_World_Cup), before becoming the first English club to claim three consecutive league titles twice. In [2010–11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010%E2%80%9311_Manchester_United_F.C._season), Manchester United won their 19th top division title, passing [Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_F.C.)'s previous record of 18, before winning a 20th title in [2012–13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012%E2%80%9313_Manchester_United_F.C._season). In [2016–17](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016%E2%80%9317_Manchester_United_F.C._season), Manchester United won their first [UEFA Europa League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Europa_League) trophy, making them only the fifth club to win the [three main European club competitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_club_competition_records_and_statistics#List_of_teams_to_have_won_the_three_main_European_club_competitions).

5. 25 goals in the Premier League

8. Joint top scorer with [Jimmy Floyd Hasselbaink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Floyd_Hasselbaink) of [Leeds United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeds_United_A.F.C.) and [Michael Owen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Owen) of [Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_F.C.)
9. The 22 top English clubs broke away from [the Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_League) to form the [FA Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Premier_League) as the new top tier of the [English football pyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_football_league_system).
10. Manchester United were deducted one point after [a brawl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_United_F.C.%E2%80%93Arsenal_F.C._brawl_(1990)) in a game with [Arsenal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arsenal_F.C.) on 20 October 1990.
11. The [1981–82 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1981%E2%80%9382_in_English_football) saw the introduction of three points for a win.
12. 28 goals in the First Division
13. Joint top scorer with [Ron Davies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Davies_(footballer,_born_1942)) of [Southampton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southampton_F.C.)
14. From 1939 to 1993, in the event of a draw, the [Charity Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Community_Shield) was shared between the two competing teams, with each team having possession of the trophy for six months.
15. Despite entering the first [League Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Cup) in [1960–61](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1960%E2%80%9361_Football_League_Cup), like many other major clubs, Manchester United declined to take part again until the [1966–67](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1966%E2%80%9367_Football_League_Cup) season. They then missed two consecutive seasons in [1967–68](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1967%E2%80%9368_Football_League_Cup) and [1968–69](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1968%E2%80%9369_Football_League_Cup).
16. 32 goals in the First Division
17. The FA Cup was contested in [1945–46](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1945%E2%80%9346_FA_Cup) but the Football League did not resume until the following season.
18. No competitive football was played between 1939 and 1946 due to the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II).
19. The [1939–40 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1939%E2%80%9340_Football_League) was abandoned in early September and all results annulled, after only three matches had been played; Manchester United were tenth in the table at the time.
20. Lowest League finish
21. No competitive football was played between 1915 and 1919 due to the [First World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I).
22. For finishing in 2nd place, Newton Heath had to play Test matches against [Burnley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burnley_F.C.) and [Sunderland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunderland_A.F.C.) in order to gain promotion to Division One. They beat Burnley over two legs, but lost to Sunderland and remained in Division Two.
23. As a reward for finishing in 3rd place, Newton Heath played yet another Test match against the team that finished third from bottom in the First Division, [Stoke City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stoke_City_F.C.). They lost, and remained in the Second Division.
24. Formal promotion and relegation had still not been introduced, and so another Test match was played between Newton Heath and [Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_F.C.), the winners of the Second Division. Newton Heath lost, and were relegated.
25. Formal promotion and relegation had not yet been established, and so the bottom team in the Football League First Division would play a so-called " [Test match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Playoffs#England)" against the top team in the [Second Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Second_Division), [Small Heath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham_City_F.C.). Newton Heath drew the original Test match 1–1, then won the replay 5–2, and retained their place in the First Division.
26. In 1892, the [Football Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_Alliance) and the Football League decided to merge. Due to their second place finish in the [1891–92 Football Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1891%E2%80%9392_Football_Alliance), Newton Heath were elected to the [Football League First Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_First_Division) for the following season.
27. The 1888–89 season was cut short for Newton Heath, as the Combination was wound up in April 1889, and so the team was unable to complete its programme of 16 matches. However, records show that the club had the best record of all the teams in the league at that point.
28. UEFA club competitions were first contested in 1955–56.
29. The Charity Shield, later renamed the [FA Community Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Community_Shield), was first held in 1908.
30. The Football League Cup, later renamed the [EFL Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup) was first contested in 1960–61.
31. The club did not start playing league football until 1888.
32. Goals in all competitions ([Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_League) or [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League), [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup), [EFL Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup), [European](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA#Competitions) and [FA Community Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Community_Shield)) are counted.
