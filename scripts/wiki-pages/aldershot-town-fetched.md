# Content from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Aldershot_Town_F.C._seasons

List of Aldershot Town F.C. seasons

[Aldershot Town Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aldershot_Town_F.C.), is a professional [association football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) club based in [Aldershot, Hampshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aldershot), England, was founded in 1992 after the closure of debt-ridden [Aldershot Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aldershot_F.C.).

## Key

Key to positions and symbols

- * = Qualified for the play-offs
- ↓ = [Relegated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_and_relegation)
- ↑ = [Promoted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_and_relegation)
- 2nd = Runners-up
- 1st = Champions

Key to rounds

- W = Winners
- RU = Runners-up
- SF = Semi-finals
- QF = Quarter-finals
- R1 = First round, etc.
- QR1 = First qualifying round, etc.
- QR = Qualifying round
