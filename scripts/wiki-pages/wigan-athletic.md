List of Wigan Athletic F.C. seasons

The [DW Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DW_Stadium)(formerly the JJB Stadium) has been the home of Wigan Athletic since the 1999–2000 season.

[Wigan Athletic Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wigan_Athletic_F.C.) was founded in 1932 and joined [the Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_League) in 1978. The team played in the [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League) for the first time in [2005–06](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005%E2%80%9306_in_English_football). The table details the club's achievements in all [first team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_team_(association_football)) competitions, and records their top goalscorer, for each completed season.
