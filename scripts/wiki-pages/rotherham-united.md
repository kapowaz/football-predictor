List of Rotherham United F.C. seasons

Rotherham United's league performance from their first season to the [2019–20 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_Rotherham_United_F.C._season).

[Rotherham United Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotherham_United_F.C.) is an [association football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) club based in [Rotherham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotherham), [South Yorkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Yorkshire). The club was formed in 1925 as a merger between [Rotherham Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotherham_Town_F.C._(1899)) and [Rotherham County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotherham_County_F.C.) when it was decided that having two professional clubs in the town was not sustainable. After the merger, the club was elected to play in the [Football League Third Division North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Third_Division_North). United played in the Third Division North until [1950–51](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1950%E2%80%9351_in_English_football), when they were champions of the division and were promoted to the [Second Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Second_Division). During their first spell in the Second Division the club reached the fifth round of the [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup) twice and were runners up in the inaugural [Football League Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup), which is the furthest they have reached in these competitions. Their furthest [Football League Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Trophy) run saw the club win the competition twice, in the [1995–96](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995%E2%80%9396_Football_League_Trophy) season defeating [Shrewsbury Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrewsbury_Town_F.C.) 2–1 in [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1996_Football_League_Trophy_Final); and in the [2021–22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_EFL_Trophy) season defeating [Sutton United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sutton_United_F.C.) 4–2 after extra time in [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_EFL_Trophy_Final).

As of the end of [2022–23](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_in_English_football), the club's first team has remained in the [English Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Football_League) since its formation. Their highest finish came in [1954–55](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1954%E2%80%9355_in_English_football) when they finished in third place in the Second Division, missing out on promotion to the top division only on [goal average](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goal_average). In total the club has spent 27 seasons in the second tier of the [English football league system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_football_league_system), 47 in the third and 13 in the fourth. Rotherham has qualified for four [Football League play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Football_League_play-offs), in [1998–99](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998%E2%80%9399_in_English_football) they lost in the Third Division semi-final to [Leyton Orient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leyton_Orient_F.C.), in [2009–10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009%E2%80%9310_in_English_football) they reached the [League Two final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Football_League_Two_play-off_final) but lost 3–2 to [Dagenham & Redbridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dagenham_&amp;_Redbridge_F.C.), in [2013–14](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013%E2%80%9314_in_English_football) they reached the [League One final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Football_League_One_play-off_final) and defeated Leyton Orient in a [penalty shoot-out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_shoot-out_(association_football)) to gain promotion the [Football League Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Championship), and in [2017–18](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017%E2%80%9318_in_English_football) they again reached the [League One final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_EFL_League_One_play-off_final) and defeated [Shrewsbury Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrewsbury_Town_F.C.) 2–1 to gain promotion the EFL Championship. The table details their achievements in [first-team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossary_of_association_football_terms#F) competitions for each completed season since their first appearance in the Football League in 1925–26.

## Notes

1. The 2019–20 season was curtailed due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_the_United_Kingdom), with league positions decided on a points-per-game basis. Rotherham United averaged 1.77 points from 35 games.
2. Promoted into the [EFL Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Championship) via the play-offs, beating [Scunthorpe United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scunthorpe_United_F.C.) 4–2 on aggregate in the semi-final and [Shrewsbury Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrewsbury_Town_F.C.) 2–1 in the [2018 EFL League One play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_EFL_League_One_play-off_final).
3. Three points deducted for fielding an ineligible player.
4. Promoted into the [Football League Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Championship) via the play-offs, beating [Preston North End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preston_North_End_F.C.) 4–2 on aggregate in the semi-final and [Leyton Orient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leyton_Orient_F.C.) 4–3 on [penalties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_shoot-out_(association_football)) in the [2014 Football League One play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Football_League_One_play-off_final).
5. Beat [Aldershot Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aldershot_Town_F.C.) 3–0 on aggregate in the semi-final before losing 3–2 to [Dagenham & Redbridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dagenham_&amp;_Redbridge_F.C.) in the [2010 Football League Two play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Football_League_Two_play-off_final).
6. 17 points deducted for not satisfying the normal conditions of Football League rules for exiting administration.
7. 10 points deducted for entering [administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administration_(British_football)).
8. Before the [2004–05](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004%E2%80%9305_in_English_football) season, [The Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_League) re-branded [Division One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_First_Division) as the [Football League Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Championship). [Divisions Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Second_Division) and [Three](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Third_Division) became [Leagues One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_One) and [Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Two), respectively.
9. Lost in the play-off semi-final to [Leyton Orient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leyton_Orient_F.C.) 4–2 in a penalty shootout after each leg ended 0–0.
10. The Third Division was renamed Division Two after the [FA Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League) broke away from the Football League.
11. The FA Cup was contested in [1945–46](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1945%E2%80%9346_in_English_football) but the Football League did not resume until the following season.
12. When the Second World War began, the 1939–40 Football League season was abandoned with three matches played.
13. Includes goals scored in league games (including play-offs), [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup), [EFL Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup) and [EFL Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Trophy).
