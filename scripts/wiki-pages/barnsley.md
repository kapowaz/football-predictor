List of Barnsley F.C. seasons

Barnsley performances from 1898 until 2025

[Barnsley Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnsley_Football_Club) is an English [association football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) club based in the [South Yorkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Yorkshire) town of [Barnsley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnsley). Founded in 1887 under the name Barnsley St Peter's, the team played in the [Sheffield & District League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield_&amp;_District_Football_League) from the 1890–91 season and first entered the [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup) in [1893–94](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1893%E2%80%9394_FA_Cup). Two years later, they were accepted into the [Midland League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midland_Football_League_(1889)). The club changed its name to Barnsley F.C. in 1897; its team finished as Midland League runners-up in the first season under the new name and were elected to the newly expanded [Second Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Second_Division) of the [Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Football_League) for the [1898–99 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1898%E2%80%9399_Football_League). A 16th-place finish in their second season meant they had to apply for [re-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Re-election_(Football_League)); the application was successful, and Barnsley continued safely in mid-table until 1911, when they again needed to be re-elected to the League. Their cup form was rather better: either side of that poor League placing, they reached the FA Cup final. In [1909–10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1909%E2%80%9310_FA_Cup), they drew 1–1 with [Newcastle United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_United) in [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1910_FA_Cup_Final) at [Crystal Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal_Palace_National_Sports_Centre#Football), but lost 1–0 in the [replay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replay_(sports)) at [Everton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everton_F.C.)'s [Goodison Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodison_Park) ground. Two seasons later, after taking three replays to get through the quarter-final, they played out a goalless draw with [West Bromwich Albion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bromwich_Albion) at Crystal Palace; this time Barnsley won the replay, at [Sheffield United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield_United_F.C.)'s [Bramall Lane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bramall_Lane) ground, [by one goal to nil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1912_FA_Cup_final).

In the remaining years before competitive football was suspended for the duration of the First World War, Barnsley established themselves as one of the stronger sides in the Second Division, placing third in 1914–15. The [First Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_First_Division) was to be expanded by two teams for the first post-war season. Traditionally, existing top-flight teams were reprieved from relegation when such an expansion took place, but when the League chose to relegate [Tottenham Hotspur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tottenham_Hotspur), who had finished bottom, Barnsley had expectations of promotion. Instead of promoting the top three from the Second Division, the top two went up and the League opted to ballot its members as to the third candidate; [Arsenal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arsenal_F.C.), who had finished fifth in 1915, won the ballot. Barnsley missed out on promotion in 1921–22 on [goal average](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goal_average), and continued in the Second Division until 1931–32 when they went down to the [Third Division North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Third_Division_North), also on goal average. They returned to the second tier as champions two years later, were relegated in 1938, and won another Third Division title in 1939.

After relegation in 1953, Barnsley won the Third Division championship for a third time in 1955, but ten years later they were in the [Fourth Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Fourth_Division). They moved between fourth and third tiers before two promotions in three years took them back to the Second Division in 1981, in which they remained for the next sixteen seasons. With two matches left to play in the 1996–97 season, to the accompaniment of chants of "It's just like watching Brazil", [Danny Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Wilson_(footballer,_born_1960)) had managed Barnsley to within one win of promotion to the [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League). At home to [Bradford City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradford_City_A.F.C.), [Paul Wilkinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Wilkinson_(footballer)) gave them a first-half lead, and with three minutes to go, [Clint Marcelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clint_Marcelle) scored the goal that made sure of the win. Barnsley's visit to the top flight was brief; they finished 19th, five points short of safety. They came close to a return in 2000 via the [play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_Football_League_play-offs#Division_One), losing 4–2 in [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_Football_League_First_Division_play-off_final) after [Ipswich Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ipswich_Town_F.C.)'s goalkeeper had saved a [penalty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_kick_(association_football)) and made a late save to deny them an equaliser.

Two years later, they were relegated to the third tier, which combined with the loss of revenue following the failure of [ITV Digital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITV_Digital) and its broadcasting deal with the Football League made Barnsley one of some thirty clubs driven into [administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administration_(British_football)). They recovered, and went on to beat [Swansea City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swansea_City_A.F.C.) in a [penalty shoot-out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_shoot-out_(association_football)) in the [2006 play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Football_League_One_play-off_final) and return to the second tier, by then renamed the [Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Championship). Despite finishing no higher than 17th place, they retained their second-tier status for eight seasons. Relegated in 2014, they returned via the play-offs, beating [Millwall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millwall_F.C.) 3–1 in the [2016 final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Football_League_One_play-off_final). Also in 2015–16, Barnsley beat [Oxford United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_United) 3–2 in [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Football_League_Trophy_Final) to win the [Football League Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Trophy), a [cup competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cup_competition) open to teams from the lower two divisions of the Football League, for the first time. They were again relegated in 2017–18, but made an immediate return to the Championship, and in [2020–21](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_EFL_Championship), reached the play-offs, in which they lost to [Swansea City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swansea_City_A.F.C.) in the semi-final. In finishing bottom of the [2021–22 Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_EFL_Championship), they recorded the second lowest number of wins and points (adjusted for [three points for a win](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_points_for_a_win)) in the club's history, won the fewest away matches since 1930, scored the fewest goals for 50 years and set a club record for fewest scored at home. The following season, they reached the play-offs, losing to [Sheffield Wednesday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield_Wednesday) in [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_English_Football_League_play-offs#League_One) to a goal scored with six seconds of extra time remaining. In the [2023–24 FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_FA_Cup), Barnsley were taken to a replay by seventh-tier team [Horsham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horsham_F.C.) which they won 3–0, but were then disqualified for fielding an ineligible player.

As of the end of the 2024–25 season, Barnsley have spent 10 seasons in the fourth tier of the [English football league system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_football_league_system), 27 in the third, 78 in the second and 1 in the top tier. ## Notes

1. After drawing 3–3 at home to [Isthmian League Premier Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isthmian_League_Premier_Division)(seventh-tier) club [Horsham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horsham_F.C.) in the [FA Cup first round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_FA_Cup#First_round), Barnsley won the replay 3–0. They were then disqualified for fielding an ineligible player, and Horsham reinstated.
2. Lost 5–4 on aggregate to [Bolton Wanderers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolton_Wanderers) in the [play-off semi-final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_English_Football_League_play-offs#League_One).
3. After beating [Bolton Wanderers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolton_Wanderers) 2–1 on aggregate in the [play-off semi-final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_English_Football_League_play-offs#League_One), lost 1–0 to [Sheffield Wednesday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield_Wednesday) in [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_EFL_League_One_play-off_final).
4. Lost 2–1 on aggregate to [Swansea City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swansea_City_A.F.C.) in the [play-off semi-final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_English_Football_League_play-offs#Championship).
5. Beat [Oxford United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_United) 3–2 in [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Football_League_Trophy_Final) to win the Football League Trophy for the first time.
6. After beating [Walsall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walsall_F.C.) 6–1 on aggregate in the [play-off semi-final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Football_League_play-offs#League_One), beat [Millwall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millwall_F.C.) 3–1 in [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Football_League_One_play-off_final) to gain promotion to the Championship.
7. After beating [Huddersfield Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huddersfield_Town_A.F.C.) 3–2 on aggregate in the [play-off semi-final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Football_League_play-offs#League_One), beat [Swansea City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swansea_City_A.F.C.) in [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Football_League_One_play-off_final) in a [penalty shoot-out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_shoot-out_(association_football)) to gain promotion to the Championship.
8. From the 2004–05 season, the Football League divisions were rebranded: Division One was renamed the [Football League Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Championship), and Divisions Two and Three became [Football League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_One) and [Football League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Two) respectively.
9. After beating [Birmingham City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham_City_F.C.) 5–2 on aggregate in the [play-off semi-final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_Football_League_play-offs#First_Division), lost 4–2 to [Ipswich Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ipswich_Town_F.C.) in [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_Football_League_First_Division_play-off_final).
10. When the newly formed [FA Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Premier_League) split from the Football League, the remaining divisions of the Football League were renumbered upwards.
11. RSSSF list [David Currie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Currie_(footballer)) as 1987–88 divisional top scorer with 28 goals, but 21 of those were scored for [Darlington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darlington_F.C.) in the [Fourth Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Fourth_Division) before he joined Barnsley.
12. The 1981–82 season saw the introduction of [three points for a win](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_points_for_a_win) instead of two.
13. The English National Football Archive general source gives Joicey 26 league goals in 1976–77 while RSSSF list him as divisional top scorer with 25.
14. The [Football League Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Cup) competition started in the [1960–61](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1960%E2%80%9361_in_English_football) season.
15. Although the Football League did not resume until the 1946–47 season, the FA Cup was contested in 1945–46. From the first round proper to the sixth round (quarter-final), results were determined on aggregate score [over two legs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-legged_tie).
16. The [1939–40](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1939%E2%80%9340_in_English_football) season was abandoned with three matches played when the Second World War began.
17. Clubs reaching the third round of the [1938–39 FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1938%E2%80%9339_FA_Cup) were excused from entering the Northern Section Cup.
18. For the second time in three seasons, entered the competition in the first round and progressed to [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1912_FA_Cup_Final). They needed three replays to get past [Bradford City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradford_City_A.F.C.) in the quarter-final. They played out a goalless draw with [West Bromwich Albion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bromwich_Albion) at Crystal Palace, and this time won the replay, at [Sheffield United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield_United_F.C.)'s [Bramall Lane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bramall_Lane) ground, by one goal to nil.
19. Entered the competition in the first round and progressed to [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1910_FA_Cup_Final) at [Crystal Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal_Palace_National_Sports_Centre#Football), in which they drew 1–1 with [Newcastle United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_United). In the [replay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replay_(sports)), they lost 1–0 at [Everton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everton_F.C.)'s [Goodison Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodison_Park) ground.
20. Successfully applied for [re-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Re-election_(Football_League)) to the Football League.
21. The Central Division of the Sheffield & District League was combined with the [Wharncliffe Charity Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wharncliffe_Charity_Cup). The club chose not to enter the Midland League.
22. Football League divisions are sorted according to their level within the [English football league system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_football_league_system), and separately from non-League divisions.
23. Seasons from 1898–99 to 2002–03 sourced to the English National Football Archive; seasons from 2003–04 onwards sourced to Soccerbase.
