It details the club's achievements in major competitions, the top scorers in all competitions, and the average home league attendance for each season.

## Footnotes

1. Performed the greatest comeback in EFL play-off history to beat [Peterborough United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peterborough_United_F.C.) in the semi-final, then beat [Barnsley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnsley_F.C.) 1-0 in extra time in the [play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_EFL_League_One_play-off_final).
2. Lost in the play-off semi-final to [Sunderland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunderland_A.F.C.) 2–1 on aggregate.
3. All home games in the 2020–21 season were played behind closed doors due to the COVID-19 pandemic.
4. Sheffield Wednesday received a 12 point deduction for breaching the League's Profitability and Sustainability Rules. This was later reduced to 6 points by an Independent League Arbitration panel.
5. Lost in the play-off semi-final to [Huddersfield Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huddersfield_Town_A.F.C.) 4–3 in a [penalty shoot-out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_shoot-out_(association_football)) after the aggregate score was 1–1.
6. Beat [Brighton & Hove Albion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brighton_&amp;_Hove_Albion_F.C.) 3–1 on aggregate in the semi-final before losing 1–0 to [Hull City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hull_City_A.F.C.) in the [2016 Football League Championship play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Football_League_Championship_play-off_final).
7. Promoted to the [Football League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_League_Two) via the play-offs, beating [Brentford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brentford_F.C.) 3–1 on aggregate in the semi-final and [Hartlepool United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartlepool_United_F.C.) 4–2 (after extra time) in the [2005 Football League One play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Football_League_One_play-off_final).
8. Lowest League finish.
9. The FA Cup was contested in [1945–46](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1945%E2%80%9346_in_English_football), but [the Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_League) did not resume until the following season.
10. When the Second World War began, the [1939–40 Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1939%E2%80%9340_Football_League) season was abandoned with three matches played.
11. The Wednesday were unanimously elected to the [First Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_First_Division) of the [Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Football_League) in 1892.
12. The Wednesday became founder members of the [Football Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_Alliance) in 1889 following their application to join [the Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_League) being turned down.
13. In the [1886–87](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1886%E2%80%9387_in_English_football) season, The Wednesday failed to submit their application for the [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup) on time, an error which ultimately led to the club turning professional later in the season.
14. League football in England was established in 1888, while Wednesday were admitted to the [Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League) in 1892.
15. League matches only (including Premier League, Football League and Football Alliance, but excluding play-offs).

17. The EFL Cup was not created until the [1960–61](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1960%E2%80%9361_in_English_football) season.
