List of AFC Wimbledon seasons

AFC Wimbledon fans and players celebrating promotion to the [Conference South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conference_South) after beating [Staines Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staines_Town_F.C.) 2–1 in the [Isthmian League Premier Division Play-off Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isthmian_League_Premier_Division) at the end of the 2007–08 season.

[AFC Wimbledon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Wimbledon) is an English professional [association football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) club, currently based in [Merton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Borough_of_Merton), [Greater London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_London). The club was formed on 30 May 2002 by supporters of [Wimbledon Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wimbledon_F.C.), led by [Kris Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kris_Stewart), Marc Jones and Trevor Williams who strongly opposed the decision of an independent commission appointed by [The Football Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_Association) to allow the [relocation of Wimbledon F.C. to Milton Keynes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relocation_of_Wimbledon_F.C._to_Milton_Keynes), to be subsequently renamed as [Milton Keynes Dons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milton_Keynes_Dons_F.C.).

AFC Wimbledon was accepted into the Premier Division of the [Combined Counties League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combined_Counties_League) for the [2002–03](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002%E2%80%9303_in_English_football) season. The club's average home attendance at league fixtures for their first season exceeded 3,000 – higher than the average attendance in the same season of Wimbledon F.C., who were still playing in the [First Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_First_Division)(now the [Football League Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Championship)). AFC Wimbledon have also set a record for the longest run of unbeaten league games at any level of men's semi-professional or professional football in the United Kingdom. The team remained unbeaten for 78 league matches between 22 February 2003 (a 2–0 defeat at home to [Withdean 2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Withdean_2000_F.C.)) and 4 December 2004 (a 2–0 defeat at [Cray Wanderers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cray_Wanderers_F.C.)).

The club went on to achieve 5 promotions in 9 seasons, ensuring that they were the first club to be formed in the 21st century to achieve Football League status, and making them the [youngest of the 72 Football League clubs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_football_clubs_in_England_and_Wales_by_year_formed). On 30 May 2016, AFC Wimbledon achieved their 6th promotion to [Football League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_One) after victory in the [2016 Football League Two play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Football_League_Two_play-off_final), exactly 14 years to the day since the club's formation.

## Notes

A. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_AFC_Wimbledon_seasons#ref_A) AFC Wimbledon were defeated 2–1 by [Fisher Athletic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fisher_Athletic_F.C.) in the Isthmian League Premier Division play-off semi-final on 2 May 2006, preventing the club from achieving three back-to-back promotions. B. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_AFC_Wimbledon_seasons#ref_B) AFC Wimbledon were deducted 3-points and fined £400 (reduced from an 18-point deduction on appeal) for the fielding of [Jermaine Darlington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jermaine_Darlington) who had not been registered correctly by the club as he required 'international clearance' in order to be transferred from the [Football Association of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_Association_of_Wales) to the [Football Association of England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_FA) and had therefore played in three matches whilst still ineligible. The 'Darlington affair' also resulted in expulsion from the Surrey Senior Cup and the FA Trophy that season. C. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_AFC_Wimbledon_seasons#ref_C) AFC Wimbledon were defeated 1–0 by [Bromley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bromley_F.C.) in the Isthmian League Premier Division play-off semi-final on 1 May 2007. D. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_AFC_Wimbledon_seasons#ref_D) AFC Wimbledon beat [Staines Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staines_Town_F.C.) 2–1 in the Isthmian League Premier Division play-off final on 3 May 2008. E. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_AFC_Wimbledon_seasons#ref_E) AFC Wimbledon beat [Luton Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luton_Town_F.C.) 4–3 in a [penalty shoot-out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_shoot-out_(association_football)) after the match had ended 0–0 in [extra time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extra_time) in the [2011 Conference National play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Conference_National_play-off_final?action=edit&amp;redlink=1) on 21 May 2011. F. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_AFC_Wimbledon_seasons#ref_F) AFC Wimbledon were deducted 3 points and given a £5,000 fine suspended for one year by a Football Disciplinary Commission hearing for the ineligible fielding of [Jake Nicholson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jake_Nicholson) in a 4–3 win over [Cheltenham Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheltenham_Town_F.C.) on 22 March 2014. In addition the club was required to pay the costs of the hearing. It was found that the player's multiplicity contract had in fact expired on 19 March 2014 and had not been renewed on that date due to the illness of a key member of the club's administrative staff. G. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_AFC_Wimbledon_seasons#ref_G) AFC Wimbledon beat [Plymouth Argyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plymouth_Argyle_F.C.) 2–0 in the [2016 Football League Two play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Football_League_Two_play-off_final) on 30 May 2016. H. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_AFC_Wimbledon_seasons#ref_H) AFC Wimbledon finished the season with a 1.00 PPG (35 points in 35 matches) which finished them in 20th position above [Tranmere Rovers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tranmere_Rovers_F.C.) by 0.06 PPG after League One voted to curtail the season following months of postponement due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic).
