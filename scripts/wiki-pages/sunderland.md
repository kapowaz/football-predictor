List of Sunderland A.F.C. seasons

Statue of former Sunderland manager [Bob Stokoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Stokoe), who took the club to their second [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup) victory in the [1972–73 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972%E2%80%9373_in_English_football)

[Sunderland Association Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunderland_A.F.C.) was founded in 1879 as Sunderland & District Teachers Association Football Club by James Allan. They turned professional in 1885. Sunderland won their first [Football League championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_League) in the [1891–92 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1891%E2%80%9392_Football_League) two years after joining the league. They won the next [Football League First Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_First_Division) on three occasions in four seasons; in [1892](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1891%E2%80%9392_Football_League), [1893](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1892%E2%80%9393_Football_League) and [1895](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1894%E2%80%9395_Football_League), separated by a runner-up spot in [1894](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1893%E2%80%9394_Football_League). In the [1901–02 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1901%E2%80%9302_Football_League), Sunderland won their fifth Football League First Division championship. They came close to completing the "league and cup double" in the [1912–13 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1912%E2%80%9313_in_English_football), winning the league but losing to [Aston Villa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aston_Villa_F.C.) in the [1913 FA Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1913_FA_Cup_final). The team's next success came in the [1935–36 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1935%E2%80%9336_Football_League) when they won the League Championship and also the [Charity Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Community_Shield). They had not won the [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup) until the [1936–37 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1936%E2%80%9337_in_English_football) when they defeated [Preston North End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preston_North_End_F.C.) in the [1937 FA Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1937_FA_Cup_final). Sunderland entered The Football League in 1890 and were not relegated from the top division until the [1957–58 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1957%E2%80%9358_in_English_football); a total of 58 seasons in the highest division of England. Their next trophy came in the [1973 FA Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1973_FA_Cup_final) as they beat [Leeds United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeds_United_F.C.) 1–0. They reached the [1985 Football League Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_Football_League_Cup_final) but finished as runners-up to [Norwich City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwich_City_F.C.) after being beaten 1–0. In the [1986–87 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986%E2%80%9387_Football_League) Sunderland were relegated to the [Football League Third Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Third_Division) for the first time in their history under the management of [Lawrie McMenemy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrie_McMenemy), they however, returned to the second division the following season as champions–their lowest position in the [English football league system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_football_league_system) until 2019. Their first appearance in the Premier League came in the [1996–97 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1996%E2%80%9397_in_English_football) after being promoted as champions from Division One. They were relegated after a single campaign but won promotion as Champions again in the 1998–99 season acquiring 105 points on the way, a second tier record at the time. Sunderland gained just 15 points in the [2005–06 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005%E2%80%9306_FA_Premier_League), which set the record for the lowest number of points in a top flight season for 3 points for a win, which has since been eclipsed by [Derby County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derby_County_F.C.).

Sunderland have won the [League Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_football_champions) six times, the FA Cup twice, and the [Charity Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Community_Shield) three times (including the [Sheriff of London Charity Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheriff_of_London_Charity_Shield)). They have been runners-up in the League Championship five times, in the FA Cup twice and in the League Cup twice. In [European competitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA#Competitions), Sunderland have reached the second round stage of the [UEFA Cup Winners' Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Cup_Winners'_Cup). The table details the club's achievements in all national and European [first-team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_team_(association_football)) competitions, and records their top league goalscorer, for each completed season.
