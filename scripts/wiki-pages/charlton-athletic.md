List of Charlton Athletic F.C. seasons

Chart of Charlton Athletic League Performances

[Charlton Athletic Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlton_Athletic_F.C.) was founded in 1905 and turned professional in 1920. They joined the [English Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Football_League) in 1921. The club won their only [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup) in [1947](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1947_FA_Cup_Final), having finished as runners-up [12 months earlier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1946_FA_Cup_final). The table details the club's achievements in all national and European [first team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_team_(association_football)) competitions for each completed season.
