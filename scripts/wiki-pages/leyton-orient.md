List of Leyton Orient F.C. seasons

This is a list of all the seasons played by [Leyton Orient Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leyton_Orient_F.C.) in [English football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_in_England). The club was formed in 1881 as Glyn Cricket Club, and started a football section named Orient Football Club in 1888. Friendly matches were played against local sides until the club was elected into the Clapton & District League for the 1893–94 season.

Leyton Orient is the second oldest football club in London, behind [Fulham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulham_F.C.), and was elected to [The Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Football_League) for the 1905–06 season. The club has undergone a number of name changes during its history: from Orient to Clapton Orient in 1898; to Leyton Orient in 1946; a reversion to Orient in 1966, then a return to the Leyton Orient name in 1987. The club has reached the top flight of English football once in its history, a single season in Division One in 1962–63 which ended in relegation. Leyton Orient's best performance in the [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup) was in reaching the semi-final stage in 1977–78.

This list includes the abandoned 1939–40 season and the unofficial Second World War leagues.
