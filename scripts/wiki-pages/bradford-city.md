List of Bradford City A.F.C. seasons

The [Bradford City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradford_City_A.F.C.) side that won the [1911 FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1911_FA_Cup_Final)—the only major honour to be won by the club

[Bradford City Association Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradford_City_A.F.C.) were formed in 1903 and were elected into the [Second Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Second_Division) before they had even played a game. Bradford City and [Chelsea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chelsea_F.C.), in 1905, remain the only teams to be elected into the league before playing a competitive fixture. The club were promoted to the [First Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_First_Division) when they won the Second Division title in [1907–08](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1907%E2%80%9308_Bradford_City_A.F.C._season). In [1910–11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1910%E2%80%9311_Bradford_City_A.F.C._season) the club recorded their highest league position of fifth and also won their only major honour when captain [Jimmy Speirs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Speirs) lifted the [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup) after he scored the only goal to defeat [Newcastle United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_United_F.C.) 1–0 in the [final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1911_FA_Cup_Final) replay. Bradford City's honours also include the [Third Division North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Third_Division_North) title in [1928–29](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1928%E2%80%9329_Bradford_City_A.F.C._season) and the [Third Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Third_Division) crown in [1984–85](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1984%E2%80%9385_Bradford_City_A.F.C._season), as well as the [Third Division North Challenge Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Third_Division_North_Cup) in [1938–39](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1938%E2%80%9339_Bradford_City_A.F.C._season).

Bradford City have spent two seasons in the [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League). In [1999–2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1999%E2%80%932000_Bradford_City_A.F.C._season), they avoided relegation with just 36 points, then a record low to stay up, after defeating [Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_F.C.) 1–0 in the final game with a headed goal from [David Wetherall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Wetherall). The club have won promotion in a total of eight seasons and been relegated on ten occasions; three relegations in the previous seven seasons meant the [2007–08](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007%E2%80%9308_Bradford_City_A.F.C._season) season was their first in the [fourth tier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Two) in 26 years. Bradford City have also played in European competition when they took one of England's two places in the [Intertoto Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Intertoto_Cup) in [2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_UEFA_Intertoto_Cup). They defeated [FK Atlantas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FK_Atlantas) and [RKC Waalwijk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RKC_Waalwijk) before being knocked out by [FC Zenit Saint Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Zenit_Saint_Petersburg) in the semi-finals.
