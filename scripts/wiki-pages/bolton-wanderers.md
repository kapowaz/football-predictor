List of Bolton Wanderers F.C. seasons Bolton Wanderers league performances

This is a list of league seasons played by [Bolton Wanderers Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolton_Wanderers_F.C.) in English and European football, from 1888 to the current season. The club was founded in 1874 by the Reverend John Farrall Wright.

The club has won the FA Cup four times and the Charity Shield once. They have competed in the [UEFA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Europa_League) on two occasions.

The club's highest league position is third in the [First Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_First_Division), achieved three times, in 1891–92, 1920–21 and 1924–25. Their lowest league position is third in the [fourth tier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_League_Two)(Fourth Division and League Two), twice in 1987–88 and 2020–21, the only seasons they competed in the lowest professional tier.
