This is a record of every completed season of [Blackpool Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackpool_F.C.) in [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_in_England) and [European](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA#Competitions) [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_(soccer)), from 1896 to 1897, their first season in the [Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_League), to their current campaign. It details the club's achievements in major competitions and the top scorers in the League for each season.

Note: The 1945–46 post-War season statistics are not recognised officially.

## Footnotes

1. The 2019–20 season was curtailed due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_the_United_Kingdom), with league positions decided on a points-per-game basis. Blackpool averaged 1.29 points from 35 games.
2. Divisions One, Two and Three were renamed Football League Championship, Football League One and Football League Two, respectively.
3. After formation of the [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League), the [Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_League) restructured and Division Three became Division Two.
4. The two Football League matches against [Aldershot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aldershot_F.C.) in 1991–92 were expunged from the record books when Aldershot went out of business that season.
5. Lowest-ever League finish.
6. Deducted two points for fielding an ineligible player.
7. The 1981–82 season saw the introduction of three points for a win.
8. Highest-ever League finish.
9. The 1945–46 post-War season statistics are not recognised officially.
10. The 1939–40 season was abandoned due to the outbreak of the Second World War. Regional competitions were implemented during this time.
11. League goals only.
12. The EFL Trophy was first contested in the 1983–84 season.
13. The EFL Cup was first contested in the 1960–61 season.
