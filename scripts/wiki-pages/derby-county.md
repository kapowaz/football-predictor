## Notes

1. Derby were deducted 21 points, 12 for entering [administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administration_(British_football))& 9 for breaking EFL accountancy rules.
2. Promoted via the play-offs.
3. At the beginning of the 2004–05 season, the Football League renamed its divisions: the First Division (which Derby were playing in) became The Championship, the Second Division became League One and the Third Division became League Two.
4. At the beginning of the 1992–93 season, the clubs in the Football League First Division broke away to form the FA Premier League; as a result, the Football League's three remaining divisions were renamed: the Second Division (which Derby were playing in) became the First Division, the Third Division became the Second Division and the Fourth Division became the Third Division.
5. Had English clubs not been banned from competing in European competition, Derby would have taken part in the UEFA Cup in the 1989–90 season.
6. Lowest League finish.
7. At the beginning of the 1958–59 season, the Football League's Third Division (North) and Third Division (South) were replaced by a national Third Division and a national Fourth Division; therefore, Derby competed in the national Third Division upon relegation in 1984.
8. At the beginning of the 1981–82 season, the Football League changed the points system from two to [three points for a win](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_points_for_a_win).
9. Derby qualified for the Fairs Cup, but were banned from competing due to financial irregularities.
10. At the beginning of the 1892–93 season, the Football League expanded to include a second division; the existing division (which Derby were playing in) was renamed the First Division.
11. Goals in all competitions ([English Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Football_League) or [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League), [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup), [EFL Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup), [EFL Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Trophy), [Charity Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Community_Shield) and [European](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA#Competitions)) are counted.
12. The EFL Cup was founded in 1961.
