[Chesterfield Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chesterfield_Football_Club) is an English [association football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) club based in the [Derbyshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derbyshire) town of Chesterfield. The current club dates from 1919, but the histories of earlier Chesterfield-based clubs are generally discussed alongside that of the current club. The first Chesterfield F.C. was founded in the mid-1860s and survived until 1881, long before league football existed. The second incarnation, which became known as Chesterfield Town F.C., was formed in 1884. Its first team joined and won the [Sheffield & District League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield_&amp;_District_Football_League) in 1891–92, and first entered the [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup) the [following season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1892%E2%80%9393_FA_Cup). Drawn away to [Gainsborough Trinity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gainsborough_Trinity) in the first qualifying round, the score stood at 2–2 at full time; Trinity scored twice during [extra time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extra_time), but darkness fell before the agreed half hour was complete, so the match was ordered to be replayed. Chesterfield lost the [replay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replay_(sports)) 4–0. They continued in the Sheffield leagues until joining the [Midland Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midland_Football_League_(1889)) in 1896. After three top-four finishes, Chesterfield were elected to the [Second Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Second_Division) of [the Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_League) for the [1899–1900 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1899%E2%80%931900_Football_League). The team generally struggled in the Football League: they placed fifth in 1904–05, but followed up with four consecutive bottom-three positions. Three times they successfully applied for [re-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Re-election_(Football_League)) to the League, but the fourth application failed, and Chesterfield returned to the Midland League in 1909. Despite winning the title that season, they were not accepted back into the Football League, and continued in the Midland League until competitive football was suspended for the duration of the First World War.

Chesterfield Town went into [voluntary liquidation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voluntary_liquidation) in 1915. A third club, formed to participate in the wartime competitions, failed because of financial irregularities, and when competitive football resumed, [Chesterfield Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_Borough_of_Chesterfield) founded its own club, Chesterfield Municipal. They won the first post-war Midland League title – and were expelled from that season's FA Cup for fielding an ineligible player – but when the football authorities made clear their opposition to municipal ownership of clubs, the council's involvement ceased and the club was renamed Chesterfield F.C. It was a founder member of the [Football League Third Division North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Third_Division_North), spent much of the 1920s in the top half of that division, and won the title in [1930–31](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1930%E2%80%9331_Football_League#Third_Division_North). They retained their Second Division status for just two seasons, won a further Third Division North championship in 1936, and remained in the second tier until the interruption for war and for five seasons thereafter. Consistent top-half finishes meant Chesterfield were allocated to the [Third Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Third_Division) when the regional sections were amalgamated into Third and [Fourth Divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Fourth_Division) in 1958, but they lasted only three seasons before making their debut at the lower level.

Chesterfield were promoted as 1969–70 Fourth Division champions. In 1980–81, they entered and won the last edition of the [Anglo-Scottish Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Scottish_Cup), defeating Scottish giants [Rangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rangers_F.C.) 3–0 in the quarter-finals before beating [Notts County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notts_County) in the final. Two years later, they were relegated, and came close to folding, with debts of £400,000 and only seven contracted professionals, before bouncing back to win their second Fourth Division title in 1985. Over the next 30 years, they experienced four relegations and four promotions between third and fourth tiers. Promoted via the [play-offs in 1995](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995_Football_League_play-offs#Third_Division), they came within a point of the [play-offs the following season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1996_Football_League_play-offs#Second_Division), and reached the semi-final of the [FA Cup in 1996–97](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1996%E2%80%9397_FA_Cup), in which they faced [Middlesbrough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middlesbrough_F.C.) of the [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League). With 21 minutes left and Chesterfield leading 2–1, [Jonathan Howard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Howard_(footballer)) shot against the underside of the bar; the ball bounced down and then out, and referee [David Elleray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Elleray) ruled, incorrectly, that it had not crossed the line. A minute later, he awarded a penalty to Middlesbrough for a foul that took place outside the penalty area. Middlesbrough scored, and took a 3–2 lead in [extra time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extra_time), but [Jamie Hewitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamie_Hewitt_(footballer)) equalised with seconds left. Chesterfield lost the [replay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replay_(sports)) 3–0.

In 2000–01, a nine-point deduction for financial irregularities, initially deemed too lenient by the Football League, failed to prevent Chesterfield's automatic promotion in third place, and the following year the club was taken out of [administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administration_(British_football)) into the ownership and control of the Chesterfield Football Supporters' Society. In the second round of the 2008–09 FA Cup, Chesterfield lost to [non-league](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-league) club [Droylsden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Droylsden_F.C.), but were awarded the tie when their opponents were found to have fielded an ineligible player; when Chesterfield did the same in 2014–15, they were only required to replay the tie, and won. They again won the fourth-tier title in 2010–11 and [the following season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011%E2%80%9312_Football_League_Trophy) won the [Football League Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Trophy), a [cup competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cup_competition) open to teams from the two lower divisions of the Football League, beating [Swindon Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swindon_Town_F.C.) 2–0 in [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Football_League_Trophy_Final). Chesterfield won the fourth-tier championship for a league record fourth time in [2013–14](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013%E2%80%9314_Football_League_Two), and again reached the [Football League Trophy final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Football_League_Trophy_Final); this time they lost 3–1 to [Peterborough United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peterborough_United). Two consecutive bottom-of-the-table finishes took them back to non-league football in 2018. After two successive defeats in the play-offs, they came closest to a return to League football in 2023 when losing [on penalties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_shoot-out_(association_football)) to [Notts County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notts_County) in the [play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_National_League_play-off_final).

Since their first admission to the Football League in 1899, Chesterfield have spent 20 seasons in the second tier of the [English football league system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_football_league_system), 55 in the third, 25 in the fourth and 13 in non-league football. ## Notes

1. Lost to 4th place [Walsall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walsall_F.C.) in the [play-off semi-finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_English_Football_League_play-offs).
2. After beating [Bromley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bromley_F.C.) after extra time in the semi-final, lost [on penalties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_shoot-out_(association_football)) to second-placed [Notts County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notts_County) in the [play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_National_League_play-off_final).
3. The [third-round tie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_FA_Trophy#Third_round_proper) against [Guiseley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guiseley_A.F.C.) was called off after Chesterfield players tested positive for COVID-19. As in the previous season, the club opted to withdraw from the competition.
4. Lost to third-placed [Solihull Moors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solihull_Moors_F.C.) in the [play-off semi-finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_National_League#Play-offs).
5. Chesterfield's [fifth-round tie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_FA_Trophy#Fifth_round) against [Aldershot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aldershot_F.C.) was called off at the last minute after a player tested positive for COVID-19. The rules required it to be rescheduled within five days of the original date, but with the first-team staff in self-isolation, the club reluctantly withdrew from the competition rather than field an unprepared youth team.
6. Chesterfield's [fourth qualifying round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_FA_Cup_qualifying_rounds#Fourth_qualifying_round) win against [Stockport County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockport_County) was ordered to be replayed because they fielded an ineligible player. Stockport won the replay 4–0.
7. Lost to fifth-placed [Notts County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notts_County_F.C.) in the [play-off quarter-finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_National_League#Play-offs).
8. The 2019–20 football season was disrupted by the effects of the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_the_United_Kingdom). The National League was suspended in mid-March 2020 and the clubs voted six weeks later to end the regular season programme. Teams had not all played the same number of matches, so it was agreed to construct final league tables on an unweighted points per game basis. Chesterfield placed 20th.
9. Lost 4–0 on aggregate to [Preston North End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preston_North_End) in the [play-off semi-final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Football_League_play-offs#League_Two).
10. From the 2004–05 season, the Football League divisions were rebranded: Division One was renamed the [Football League Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Championship), and Divisions Two and Three became [Football League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_One) and [Football League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Two) respectively.
11. Nine points deducted for financial irregularities.
12. Beat [Mansfield Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mansfield_Town_F.C.) 6–3 on aggregate in the [play-off semi-final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995_Football_League_play-offs#Third_Division) before beating [Bury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bury_F.C.) 2–0 in [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995_Football_League_Third_Division_play-off_Final) to gain promotion to the Second Division.
13. When the newly formed [FA Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Premier_League) split from the Football League, the remaining divisions of the Football League were renumbered upwards.
14. Beat [Stockport County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockport_County) 6–0 [on aggregate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-legged_tie) in the [play-off semi-final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_Football_League_play-offs#Fourth_Division) before losing 1–0 to [Cambridge United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_United) in [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_Football_League_Fourth_Division_play-off_Final).
15. The [Football League Group Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Group_Cup) succeeded the [Anglo-Scottish Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Scottish_Cup) after the Scottish clubs decided to abandon the competition.
16. The 1981–82 season saw the introduction of [three points for a win](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_points_for_a_win) instead of two.
17. Chesterfield finished joint twelfth, with an identical record to that of [Tranmere Rovers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tranmere_Rovers).
18. Although the Football League did not resume until the 1946–47 season, the FA Cup was contested in 1945–46. From the first round proper to the sixth round (quarter-final), results were determined on aggregate score [over two legs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-legged_tie).
19. The [1939–40](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1939%E2%80%9340_in_English_football) season was abandoned with three matches played when the Second World War began.
20. Failed to be re-elected to the Football League, receiving three votes fewer than Midland League club [Lincoln City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_City_F.C.).
21. Successfully applied for [re-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Re-election_(Football_League)) to the Football League.
22. From the [1899–1900 Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1899%E2%80%931900_Football_League) season onwards, divisions are sorted according to their level within the [English football league system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_football_league_system), and separately from the [non-League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-League) divisions.
23. Football League seasons up to and including 1995–96 sourced to the English National Football Archive; seasons from 1996–97 onwards sourced to Soccerbase.
24. The [League Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup) competition started in the [1960–61](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1960%E2%80%9361_in_English_football) season.
25. During the period that Chesterfield took part in this competition, it was conducted on a league basis.
26. After beating [South Normanton Colliery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Normanton_Colliery_F.C.?action=edit&amp;redlink=1) in the second qualifying round, Chesterfield were disqualified for fielding an ineligible player. Although the FA commission accepted the directors' claim that they genuinely believed the player not to be [cup-tied](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cup-tied), because they suspended him for a month for "wilfully misleading the Chesterfield club", they still expelled the club from the competition despite it being within the rules and their discretion for them to impose a lesser sanction.
