List of Stockport County F.C. seasons

[Stockport County Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockport_County_F.C.), a professional [association football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) club based in [Stockport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockport), Greater Manchester, England, was founded in 1883 as Heaton Norris Rovers, they were renamed Stockport County in 1890 after the [County Borough of Stockport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Borough_of_Stockport). They joined [The Combination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Combination) for the 1891–92 season, and after three seasons they joined the [Lancashire League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancashire_League_(football)) in 1894. After six seasons in the Lancashire League, Stockport were elected to play in the [Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Football_League) in 1900 and were placed in the [Second Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Second_Division). After failing to be re-elected into the Football League in 1904 they joined the Lancashire Combination for one season before re-joining the Football League. During their time in the Football League Stockport were relegated and promoted in various divisions on 14 occasions, with the club achieving their highest league placing after finishing in 8th place of the [1997–98 Football League First Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997%E2%80%9398_Football_League_First_Division), when it was the second level of the [English football league system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_football_league_system). Stockport were relegated to the [Conference National](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_League_(division)) after finishing bottom of the League Two in the [2010–11 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010%E2%80%9311_Football_League_Two), ending 106 years of Football League membership. After 11 seasons in [non-League football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-League_football), Stockport were champions of the [2021–22 National League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_National_League) and were promoted into [EFL League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_League_Two).

Stockport County's furthest [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup) run has seen them reach the fifth round on three occasions in [1934–35](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1934%E2%80%9335_FA_Cup), [1949–50](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1949%E2%80%9350_FA_Cup) and [2000–01](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000%E2%80%9301_FA_Cup). The furthest they have reached in the [EFL Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup) is the semi-finals in the [1996–97 edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1996%E2%80%9397_Football_League_Cup). The club first participated in the [EFL Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Trophy) in 1983 when it was named the Associate Members' Cup; they were the runners-up in [1992](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Associate_Members'_Cup_Final) and [1993](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_Football_League_Trophy_final). Stockport entered four editions of the [Football League Third Division North Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Third_Division_North_Cup) between 1933 and 1937, winning the trophy in the 1934–35 season. After their exit from the Football League in 2011 Stockport were eligible to take part in the [FA Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Trophy), their furthest run in this competition is reaching the semi-final on two occasions in [2018–19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018%E2%80%9319_FA_Trophy) and [2021–22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_FA_Trophy).

## Notes

1. Lost in the play-off semi-final to [Leyton Orient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leyton_Orient_F.C.) 4–1 in a [penalty shoot-out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_shoot-out_(association_football)) after the two-legs ended 3–3 on aggregate.
2. Beat [Salford City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salford_City_F.C.) in the semi-final before losing to [Carlisle United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlisle_United_F.C.) 3–1 in a [penalty shoot-out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_shoot-out_(association_football)) in the [2023 EFL League Two play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_EFL_League_Two_play-off_final) after the match had ended 1–1 after extra time.
3. Lost in the play-off semi-final to [Hartlepool United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartlepool_United_F.C.) 1–0.
4. The 2019–20 season was disrupted by the effects of the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_England). The National League was suspended in mid-March 2020 and the clubs voted six weeks later to end the regular season programme. Teams had not all played the same number of matches, so it was agreed to construct final league tables on an unweighted points per game basis.
5. Lost in the play-off qualifying round to [Chorley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chorley_F.C.) 1–0.
6. The Football Conference was renamed the [National League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_League_(English_football)); the North and South divisions were also renamed [National League North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_League_North) and [National League South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_League_South).
7. Stockport County deducted 10 points for entering administration.
8. Promoted to [Football League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_League_One) via the play-offs, beating [Wycombe Wanderers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wycombe_Wanderers_F.C.) 2–1 on aggregate in the semi-final and [Rochdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rochdale_A.F.C.) 3–2 in the [2008 Football League Two play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Football_League_Two_play-off_final).
9. Beat [York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/York_City_F.C.) 1–0 on aggregate in the semi-final before losing 2–1 to [Burnley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burnley_F.C.) in the [1994 Football League Second Division play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_Football_League_Second_Division_play-off_final).
10. Lost in the play-off semi-final to [Port Vale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Vale_F.C.) 2–1 on aggregate.
11. The Third Division was renamed Division Two after the [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League) broke away from the Football League.
12. Beat [Stoke City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stoke_City_F.C.) 2–1 on aggregate in the semi-final before losing 2–1 to [Peterborough United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peterborough_United_F.C.) in the [1992 Football League Third Division play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Football_League_Third_Division_play-off_final).
13. Scored 23 goals in the Fourth Division.
14. Scored 30 goals in the Third Division North.
15. From the first round proper to the sixth round of the [1945–46 FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1945%E2%80%9346_FA_Cup), matches were played over two legs.
16. When the Second World War began, the [1939–40 Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1939%E2%80%9340_Football_League) season was abandoned with three matches played.
17. Scored 46 goals in the Third Division North.
18. Scored 36 goals in the Third Division North.
19. Scored 38 goals in the Third Division North.
20. Stockport County deducted 2 points for fielding an ineligible player.
21. Stockport County were elected back into the [Football League Second Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Second_Division) for the [1905–06 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1905%E2%80%9306_Football_League).
22. Stockport County were not re-elected into the [Football League Second Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Second_Division) and joined the Lancashire Combination for the 1904–05 season.
23. Stockport County were elected to the [Football League Second Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Second_Division) for the [1900–01 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1900%E2%80%9301_Football_League).
24. This is the top scorer's surname, their first name is to be confirmed.
