List of FC Halifax Town seasons

FC Halifax Town is a professional [association football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) club based in [Halifax, West Yorkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halifax,_West_Yorkshire), England. They currently compete in the [National League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_League_(division)), the fifth tier of English football, and play at [the Shay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Shay). They replaced [Halifax Town A.F.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halifax_Town_A.F.C.), which went into [administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administration_(law)) in the [2007–08 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007%E2%80%9308_in_English_football).
