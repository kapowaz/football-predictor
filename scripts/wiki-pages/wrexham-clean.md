List of Wrexham A.F.C. seasons

The Wrexham side that won the first Welsh Cup in 1878

This is a list of seasons played by [Wrexham Association Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrexham_A.F.C.) from the 1877–78 season, when the club began playing in competitive fixtures following the founding of the [Welsh Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_Cup), to the most recent current season.

The club was formed in 1864 by members of the Wrexham Cricket Club, who wanted a sporting activity for the winter months, which makes them (after [Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield_F.C.), [Cray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cray_Wanderers_F.C.), [Hallam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hallam_F.C.), [Notts County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notts_County_F.C.), and [Stoke City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stoke_City_F.C.)) the sixth oldest football team, third oldest professional club and the oldest in Wales. Their first game was played on 22 October 1864 at the Denbigh County Cricket Ground ([The Racecourse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racecourse_Ground)) against the Prince of Wales Fire Brigade.

In 1878, they won the inaugural [Welsh Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_Cup), beating [Druids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druids_F.C.) 1–0, and the club went on to win the competition a further 23 times, still the most wins by any team, until they were barred entry during the 1990s, along with other Welsh clubs playing in the [English football league system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_football_league_system). Through winning the Welsh Cup, Wrexham also qualified several times for the [UEFA Cup Winners' Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Cup_Winners'_Cup), reaching the quarter-finals during the [1975–76](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1975%E2%80%9376_European_Cup_Winners'_Cup) campaign.

In 1890, the club were founding members of the second incarnation of [The Combination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Combination). Apart from a brief two-year spell in the [Welsh Senior League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denbighshire_League) between 1894 and 1896, Wrexham spent the following fifteen seasons in The Combination, winning the competition on four occasions, before moving into the [Birmingham & District League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Midlands_(Regional)_League) in 1905. Two years after the end of the [First World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_World_War), Wrexham joined [The Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_League), where they spent eighty consecutive seasons, before being relegated to the [Conference National](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conference_National) in 2008.

Wrexham's highest position in the Football League was 15th in the old [Second Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Second_Division)(now [EFL Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Championship)) in 1978–79. Their lowest position in the League was 24th in the [Football League Fourth Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Fourth_Division) in 1965–66 (at a time when League membership maintenance was by election) and in 1990–91 (when the League decided to suspend relegation due to expansion of the [First Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_First_Division) to 22 clubs). The feat was repeated for a third time in [Football League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Two) in 2007–08 and this time they were relegated to [Conference National](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conference_National). Their lowest position in the Conference, and thus overall, was 19th in the 2019–20 season.

## Notes

In 1992-93 the [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League) was founded, and the three remaining divisions in the Football League were renamed:

- Level 4 - [Fourth Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Fourth_Division) became Third Division
- Level 3 - [Third Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Third_Division) became Second Division
- Level 2 - [Second Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Second_Division) became First Division
- Level 1 - [First Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_First_Division) became Premier League
