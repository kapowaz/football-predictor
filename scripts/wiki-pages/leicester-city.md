List of Leicester City F.C. seasons

Graph of Leicester City seasons

This is a list of seasons played by [Leicester City Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leicester_City_F.C.) in English and European [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football), from 1890 (when Leicester Fosse first competed in the [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup)) to the present day. It details the club's achievements in major competitions, and the top scorers for each season.

In 2016, Leicester City clinched their first [Premier League title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_English_football_champions), their highest position in the League. Their lowest position was in 2009, when they clinched the [League One (third tier) title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_winners_of_English_Football_League_One_and_predecessors), their only season outside the top two tiers of English football.

## Notes

1. All league fixtures bar one of the 2020–21 season were played behind closed doors due to the [COVID-19 pandemic in the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_the_United_Kingdom).
2. The remaining four league fixtures of the 2019–20 season were played behind closed doors due to the [COVID-19 pandemic in the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_the_United_Kingdom).
3. The three divisions in the Football League are re-named: Division One becomes The Championship, Division Two becomes League One and Division Three becomes League Two.
4. The 22 top English clubs break away from [The Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_League) to form the FA [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League) as the new top tier of the [English football pyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_football_league_system).
5. The 1981–82 season saw the introduction of three points for a win.
6. The FA Cup was contested in 1945–46 but The Football League did not resume until the following season.
7. League matches only.
8. Goals in all competitions ([The Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Football_League)(including [Football League play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Football_League_play-offs) or [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League), [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup), [League Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup), [Football League Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Trophy), [Anglo-Italian Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Italian_Cup), [Charity Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Community_Shield) and [European](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA#Competitions)) are counted.
9. The League Cup was founded in 1960.
