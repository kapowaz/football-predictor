List of Luton Town F.C. seasons

Luton Town Performances from 1897 until 2023

[Luton Town Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luton_Town_F.C.) is an English [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) club, founded in 1885. After becoming the first professional team in the [south of England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_of_England) in 1891, Luton joined [The Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_League) in 1897 before leaving three years later. The club rejoined the League in 1920, and reached its top division in [1955–56](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1955%E2%80%9356_in_English_football). After losing the [1959 FA Cup Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1959_FA_Cup_Final) 2–1 to [Nottingham Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nottingham_Forest_F.C.), a period of decline saw Luton in the [Fourth Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Fourth_Division) by 1965. After a swift revival the club was back in Division Two by 1970. Luton earned another [promotion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_and_relegation) four years later, returning to [Division One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_First_Division) for the [1974–75](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1974%E2%80%9375_in_English_football) season, in which Luton were [relegated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_and_relegation) back to the Second Division.

Financial crises during the 1970s led to the sale of important players and it was not until [David Pleat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Pleat), a former Luton player, was appointed in 1978 that Luton started to recover. Pleat's team achieved promotion in [1981–82](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1981%E2%80%9382_in_English_football), and remained in Division One until 1992. Luton won the [League Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_Football_League_Cup_Final) in the [1987–88](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1987%E2%80%9388_in_English_football) season with a 3–2 victory over [Arsenal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arsenal_F.C.), but it marked the beginning of decline. Inconsistent performance and financial uncertainty meant that Luton rose and fell through the divisions from season to season, and in 2007 a collapse began that would result in three successive relegations. The club claimed a [Football League Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Trophy) victory in 2009, but with it came the relegation to the [Conference Premier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conference_Premier) made inevitable by 40 points deducted in two seasons. Luton contested five years in non-League football, including three unsuccessful play-off campaigns, before winning promotion to [League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Two) as Conference Premier champions in the [2013–14](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013%E2%80%9314_Luton_Town_F.C._season) season. The club were promoted to [League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_League_One) after finishing second in League Two in [2017–18](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017%E2%80%9318_Luton_Town_F.C._season), and the [following season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018%E2%80%9319_Luton_Town_F.C._season) they won a second successive promotion to the [Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Championship) after winning the League One title. In the 2022-23 season Luton secured promotion to the Premier League by winning the Championship play off final on penalties against Coventry City at Wembley, however their stay in the top flight only lasted one season being relegated back to the Championship after a single season along with the other promoted clubs Burnley and Sheffield United. In the 2024-25 season Luton suffered a second successive relegation dropping down to League One for the first time since the 2018-19 season.
