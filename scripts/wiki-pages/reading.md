List of Reading F.C. seasons

Reading performance from 1920 until 2025

This is a list of seasons played by [Reading F.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reading_F.C.) in the [English Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Football_League). The team, established in 1871, competes in [EFL League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_League_One), the third football league in England. Established in 1871, the club first entered the [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup) in [1878–79](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1878%E2%80%9379_in_English_football) but did not play league football until the [1920–21 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1920%E2%80%9321_in_English_football).

The club has competed in all four tiers of the English Football League and has won the [Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Championship) twice, the [Football League Second Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Second_Division) three times, the [Football League Third Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Third_Division) once, reached the [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup) semi-finals twice, reached the [EFL Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup) quarter-finals twice, won the [London War Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_War_Cup), won the [Full Members Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_Members_Cup), won the [Football League Third Division South Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Third_Division_South_Cup) and won the [Berks & Bucks Senior Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berks_&amp;_Bucks_Senior_Cup) five times.

## Notes

Reading were deducted 6 points:

- Two points due to late HMRC payments.
- Three points for failing to deposit wages on time.
- One point for failing to pay players on time and in full on three occasions in the 2022–23 season.
