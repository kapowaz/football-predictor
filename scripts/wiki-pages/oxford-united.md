List of Oxford United F.C. seasons

[Oxford United Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_United_F.C.) is an English [association football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) club originally formed in 1893 under the name Headington United. The club played in local Oxfordshire leagues until being elected into the [Southern League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Football_League) in 1949. It was at this time the club turned professional. The club adopted its present name in 1960 and was elected into the [Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League) in 1962, replacing [Accrington Stanley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accrington_Stanley_F.C._(1891)). Promotion to the [Third Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Third_Division) followed in 1965, and the club were promoted again 3 years later and enjoyed an eight-year spell in the [Football League Second Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Second_Division), before being relegated. "The U's" reached the [top tier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_First_Division) of English football in 1985 after successive promotions, where they stayed for 3 years. During this time United won their only piece of major silverware, the 1986 [Football League Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Cup).

Apart from relegation to the redesignated Second Division (the old Third Division after the creation of the [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League)) and subsequent promotion shortly afterwards, Oxford United remained in the second tier for eleven years until 1999. After this a decline set in, with two relegations in three years to the [Football League Third Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Third_Division), the fourth tier of English football. In 2006 Oxford became the first club to have reached the First Division and won a major trophy to be relegated from the Football League. One of the teams to be promoted were Conference champions [Accrington Stanley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accrington_Stanley_F.C.), 44 years after Accrington lost their league status to Oxford. United gained promotion back to the Football League after beating [York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/York_City_F.C.) 3–1 in the [2010 Conference Premier play-off final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Conference_Premier_play-off_final). After six seasons in [League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_League_Two) they finished 2nd in the table and were promoted to [League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_League_One), the third tier of the English football pyramid.

## Notes

A. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_LC) The Football League Cup competition did not start until the 1960–61 season. B. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_Missing) There are no complete records of scorers until 1898 C. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_TG) Includes goals scored in the Oxon League, City Junior League, Oxfordshire District League, the Football League (including play-offs), FA Cup, Anglo-Italian Cup, Football League Cup, Associate Members' Cup/Football League Trophy, Conference National (including playoffs), FA Trophy and Conference League Cup. D. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_FA1) The FA Cup was not held until 1945 because of World War 2. E. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_Incomplete) The league records are incomplete. G. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_Spartan) Headington left the Oxfordshire Senior League and moved into the Spartan League. H. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_Southern) Headington United were elected into the Southern League. I. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_DQ) Headington United were disqualified following an appeal from Wycombe Wanderers. J. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_Fail) Despite finishing as champions, Oxford United failed to be elected into the Football League. K. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_Election) Oxford United were elected into the football league, replacing [the original Accrington Stanley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accrington_Stanley_F.C._(1891)). L. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_3pts) From the 1981–82 season, points were allocated on the basis of 3 for a win and 1 for a draw; previously only two points had been given for a win. M. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_FLT) The Football League Group Cup was renamed Football League Trophy for the 1982–83 season. The current tournament of the same name was originally the Associate Members Cup. N. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_SouthernFinal) Oxford United reached the Southern Final round, but failed to reach the overall final. O. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_NewDiv1) Division Two re-designated [Division One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_First_Division) on formation of the [F.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_Association) [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League). P. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_NewLeague2) Football League Division Three renamed " [League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Two)" because of a sponsorship change. Q. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_Play-off) After qualifying for the play-offs, Oxford United lost on penalties in the semi-finals against [Exeter City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_City_F.C.). AA. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_Weather) Only one match was played before the competition was scrapped and replaced with a Junior Cup. AB. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_WO) A match against St Paul's was a walkover. AC. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_NoTable) No table was kept as a way of ordering the teams. AD. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_WO2) A match against Cowley Juniors was a walkover. AE. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_Final) Oxford United went on to the league final, winning it in consecutive seasons. AF. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_ODL) Oxford's application to join the second division of the Oxfordshire District League was accepted. AG. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_War) Because of the Boer War, Oxford were forced to withdraw from the Oxfordshire District League as 10 members of the team were on active duty. However, the following season they returned to the league. AH. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_WO3) A match against College Servants was a walkover. AI. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_2ptspen) Oxford were deducted 2 points for fielding an illegible player. AJ. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_Crowd) After crowd trouble during a game against [Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_F.C.), Oxford were banned from playing matches within three miles of their ground, and such could not fulfill their league fixtures. AK. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_Chester_Expulsion) Only 44 games were played because of the expulsion of [Chester City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chester_City_F.C.) halfway through the season. AL. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_2010_play-off_win) Oxford were promoted through the play-offs, beating [York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/York_City_F.C.) 3–1 at Wembley Stadium. AM. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_Covid) The League one season was suspended on 3 March 2020 because of the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_the_United_Kingdom), with clubs voting to curtail the season three months later. Positions were decided based on points per game (PPG). AN. [^](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oxford_United_F.C._seasons#ref_2024_play-off_win) Oxford were promoted through the play-offs, beating [Bolton Wanderers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolton_Wanderers_F.C.) 2–0 at Wembley Stadium.
