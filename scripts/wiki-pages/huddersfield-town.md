## Notes

1. Promoted by the Play-offs at [Wembley Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wembley_Stadium), [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London).
2. Promoted by the Play-offs at (the new) [Wembley Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wembley_Stadium), [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London).
3. The [2004–05 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004%E2%80%9305_in_English_football) saw the rebranding of Divisions 1–3, so Division 1 became [The Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Championship), Division 2 became [League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_League_One)& Division 3 became [League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Two).
4. Promoted by the Play-offs at the [Millennium Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millennium_Stadium), [Cardiff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff).
5. Promoted by the Play-offs at [Wembley Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wembley_Stadium_(1923)), [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London).
6. The [1994–95 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994%E2%80%9395_in_English_football) was Town's first season at the [Kirklees Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirklees_Stadium).
7. The [1992–93 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992%E2%80%9393_in_English_football) saw the introduction of the [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League), so Division 1 replaced Division 2, Division 2 replaced Division 3 & Division 3 replaced Division 4.
8. The [1981–82 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1981%E2%80%9382_in_English_football) saw the introduction of three points for a win.
9. Lowest League finish.
10. The FA Cup was contested in [1945–46](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1945%E2%80%9346_in_English_football) but [The Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_League) did not resume until the following season.
11. The [1939–40](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1939%E2%80%9340_in_English_football) season was abandoned in early September and all results annulled, after only three matches had been played; Huddersfield Town were seventeenth in the table at the time.
12. Never played [1926 FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1926_FA_Cup_Final) Winners [Bolton Wanderers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolton_Wanderers_F.C.).
13. Never played [1925 FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1925_FA_Cup_Final) Winners [Sheffield United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield_United_F.C.).
14. Never played [1924 FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1924_FA_Cup_Final) Winners [Newcastle United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_United_F.C.).
15. The team were formed too late to enter the competition.
16. Goals in all competitions ([Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League)/ [English Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Football_League), [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup), [EFL Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup) and [EFL Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Trophy)) are counted.
