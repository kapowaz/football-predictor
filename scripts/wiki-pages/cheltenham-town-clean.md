List of Cheltenham Town F.C. seasons

[Cheltenham Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheltenham_Town_F.C.) are an English [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) club, formed in 1887. After several decades in local football, they joined the [Birmingham Combination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham_Combination) in [1932–33](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1932%E2%80%9333_in_English_football), moving to the [Southern League Western Section](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Football_League) in [1935–36](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1935%E2%80%9336_in_English_football).
