[Port Vale Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Vale_F.C.) is an English professional [association football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) club based in [Burslem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burslem), [Stoke-on-Trent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stoke-on-Trent), [Staffordshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staffordshire), who play in [EFL League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_League_One), as of the [2024–25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_EFL_League_Two) season. After becoming one of the more prominent football clubs in [Staffordshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staffordshire), Burslem Port Vale were invited to become founder members of the [Football League Second Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Second_Division) in [1892](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1892%E2%80%9393_Burslem_Port_Vale_F.C._season). They spent 13 non-consecutive seasons in the division, punctuated by two seasons in the [Midland League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midland_Football_League_(1889)) before they resigned due to financial difficulties and entered liquidation in 1907. The name of Port Vale continued in the North Staffordshire Federation League, and this new club were successful enough to be reinstated into the Football League in October 1919. They spent 16 non-consecutive seasons in the Second Division, punctuated by them winning the [Third Division North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Third_Division_North) title in [1929–30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1929%E2%80%9330_Port_Vale_F.C._season), before dropping back into the third tier for a much longer stay at the end of the 1935–36 campaign. The [1953–54](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1953%E2%80%9354_Port_Vale_F.C._season) season saw [manager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manager_(association_football)) [Freddie Steele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freddie_Steele_(footballer))'s "Iron Curtain" defence win both a Third Division North title and a semi-final place in the [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Cup). They failed to build on this success, however, though they went on to finish as champions of the first ever [Fourth Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Fourth_Division) season under [Norman Low](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Low)'s stewardship in [1958–59](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1958%E2%80%9359_Port_Vale_F.C._season).

The club had little success throughout the 1960s and 1970s, despite being briefly managed by [Stanley Matthews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Matthews), and in fact, were forced to apply for re-election after breaking [FA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_Association) rules on illegal payments in 1968. [Gordon Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordon_Lee_(footballer)) guided the club to [promotion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_and_relegation) back to the Third Division the following season, where they would remain until [relegation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_and_relegation) at the end of the 1977–78 campaign. [John McGrath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_McGrath_(footballer,_born_1938)) steered the club to promotion in 1982–83, though he departed after relegation became inevitable the following season. His assistant, [John Rudge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Rudge), became the club's longest-serving and most successful manager, leading the club from 1983 to 1999. Under his leadership Port Vale won promotions in [1985–86](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985%E2%80%9386_Port_Vale_F.C._season), [1988–89](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988%E2%80%9389_Port_Vale_F.C._season) and [1993–94](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993%E2%80%9394_Port_Vale_F.C._season), lifted the [Football League Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Trophy) in [1993](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_Football_League_Trophy_final) and reached a post-war record finish of eighth in the second tier in the [1996–97](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1996%E2%80%9397_Port_Vale_F.C._season) season. After Rudge's reign ended, the club entered a decline, slipping into the fourth tier whilst twice entering [administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administration_(British_football)) in 2003 and 2012. The decline was arrested when [Norman Smurthwaite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Smurthwaite) brought the club out of administration in 2012 and manager [Micky Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micky_Adams) achieved automatic promotion from [League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_League_Two) in the [2012–13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012%E2%80%9313_Port_Vale_F.C._season) season, though they were relegated back into League Two at the end of the [2016–17](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016%E2%80%9317_Port_Vale_F.C._season) season after a failed experiment with a continental staff and playing style. [Carol Shanahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carol_Shanahan) bought the club in 2019 and manager [Darrell Clarke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darrell_Clarke) secured promotion out of the League Two [play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Football_League_play-offs) at the end of the [2021–22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_Port_Vale_F.C._season) season, and though they were relegated back into League Two at the end of the [2023–24](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_Port_Vale_F.C._season) season, they made an immediate return to League One with [Darren Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darren_Moore) in [2024–25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Port_Vale_F.C._season).

First-team matches were recorded for the first time in 1882, meaning records go back over 140 years. [friendlies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exhibition_game) are not included in this data (including goal tallies). As of the [2025–26](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Port_Vale_F.C._season) season, Port Vale have never played top-flight football; they have spent 41 seasons in the second tier, 49 seasons in the third tier, 24 seasons in the fourth tier of the Football League, as well as 16 seasons in [non-League football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-League_football). No team has played more Football League seasons (114) without reaching the top-flight.

## Notes

1. The 2020–21 season was played [behind closed doors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behind_closed_doors_(sport)) due to the [COVID-19 pandemic in the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_the_United_Kingdom).
2. The 2019–20 season was ended early with nine fixtures left to play due to the [COVID-19 pandemic in the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_the_United_Kingdom).
3. Port Vale were forced to apply for re-election at the end of the 1967–68 season after being expelled for making illegal payments to their players.
4. The [1939–40 Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1939%E2%80%9340_Football_League) season was abandoned after three matches and the records expunged.
5. This was a wartime league with no promotion or relegation where appearances and goals were not included in official statistics.
6. An attempt was made to set up a league called [The Combination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Combination) involving clubs not invited to join [the Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Football_League). Lack of proper organisation meant it was wound up in April 1889 with many fixtures still outstanding.

11. The [EFL Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup) competition started as the Football League Cup in the [1960–61](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1960%E2%80%9361_in_English_football) season.
