List of Gillingham F.C. seasons

The Gillingham team line up before a match in the club's first season in the Football League, along with club officials and the local mayor

[Gillingham Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gillingham_F.C.) is an English [association football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) club originally formed in 1893 under the name New Brompton F.C. The club adopted its present name in 1912, and played in the [Southern League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Football_League) until 1920, when that league's top division was absorbed into the [Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League) as its new Division Three. The "Gills" were voted out of the league in favour of [Ipswich Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ipswich_Town_F.C.) at the end of the [1937–38 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1937%E2%80%9338_in_English_football), but returned 12 years later, when that league was expanded from 88 to 92 clubs.

Twice in the late 1980s Gillingham came close to winning promotion to the second tier of English football, but a decline then set in and in 1993 the club narrowly avoided relegation to the [Football Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_Conference). In 2000, Gillingham reached the second tier of the English league for the first time in the club's history. They went on to spend five seasons at this level, achieving a club record highest league finish of eleventh place in the [2002–03 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002%E2%80%9303_in_English_football) before being relegated twice to return to the league's bottom division, known since 2004 as [Football League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Two). The club has won honours at a professional level namely the [Football League Fourth Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Fourth_Division) championship in the [1963–64 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1963%E2%80%9364_in_English_football) as well as the [Football League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_Two) championship in the [2012–13 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012%E2%80%9313_in_English_football).

## Notes

1. The 2019–20 season was curtailed due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_the_United_Kingdom), with league positions decided on a points-per-game basis. Gillingham averaged 1.46 points from 35 games.
2. By finishing 5th, Gillingham reached the play-offs, then defeated [Rochdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rochdale_F.C.) 2–1 on aggregate in the semi-final, and beat [Shrewsbury Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrewsbury_Town_F.C.) 1–0 in the [final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Football_League_Two_play-off_Final) to gain promotion.
3. Gillingham finished on the same number of points as [Crewe Alexandra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crewe_Alexandra_F.C.), but were relegated on account of an inferior goal difference to the recently renamed third tier, Football League One.
4. The First Division was renamed the Football League Championship as part of a new sponsorship deal in the [2004–05](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004%E2%80%9305_in_English_football) season.
5. Gillingham finished with the same number of points as [Walsall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walsall_F.C.), but avoided relegation due to a superior goal difference.
6. This position, equivalent to 31st place in the [English football league system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_football_league_system), represents Gillingham's highest ever league finish.
7. This achievement in reaching the quarter-finals represents the furthest progress Gillingham have ever made in the FA Cup.
8. Qualifying again for the play-offs, Gillingham defeated [Stoke City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stoke_City_F.C.) 5–3 on aggregate in the semi-final, and beat [Wigan Athletic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wigan_Athletic_F.C.) 3–2 after extra time in the final to gain promotion to the second level of English football for the first time in their history.
9. Gillingham were defeated 1–0 by [Millwall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millwall_F.C.) on the [Golden goal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_goal) rule, the only time this method of resolving a match was applied to a Gillingham match.
10. Having qualified for the play-offs, Gillingham defeated [Preston North End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preston_North_End_F.C.) 2–1 on aggregate in the semi-final, and in their first appearance at [Wembley Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wembley_Stadium_(1923)) were beaten 3–1 on [penalties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_shootout_(football)) by [Manchester City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_City_F.C.), after a 2–2 draw.
11. Gillingham missed out on a place in the play-offs on goals scored, which took precedence over [goal difference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goal_difference) at that time.
12. Twenty goals remains a record for the lowest number of goals conceded in a 46 match Football League season.
13. On the formation of the FA Premier League, the Fourth Division was re-designated as the Third Division.
14. Crown also scored one goal in a match against [Aldershot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aldershot_F.C.) which was declared void when that team resigned from the league before the end of the season.
15. Gillingham qualified for the [play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_play-offs) in the first year that these were staged, defeating [Sunderland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunderland_A.F.C.) in the semi-final on away goals after a 6–6 aggregate score, and losing the final 2–0 to Swindon Town in a replay at [Selhurst Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selhurst_Park) after a 2–2 aggregate over two legs.
16. From the 1981–82 season, points were allocated on the basis of 3 for a win and 1 for a draw; previously only two points had been given for a win.
17. Gillingham finished with the same number of points as [Bournemouth & Boscombe Athletic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bournemouth_&amp;_Boscombe_Athletic_F.C.), but finished above them on goal average and thus escaped relegation.
18. Gillingham won the division on goal average.
19. Gillingham finished level on points with [Mansfield Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mansfield_Town_F.C.), but missed out on promotion on [goal average](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goal_average).
20. Having finished in the lower half of the table, Gillingham were placed in the new Football League Fourth Division following the amalgamation of the Third Division South and Third Division North.
21. Gillingham were elected into the Football League as part of the expansion of the Third Division South from 22 teams to 24.
22. Gillingham were awarded one point for an away fixture against [Millwall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millwall_F.C.) that never took place.
23. The Southern League season was abandoned after three matches due to the outbreak of war in September. Beginning in November, Gillingham played in the Kent Regional League East, which like other wartime competitions is regarded as unofficial.
24. Gillingham failed to be re-elected having finished in last position, and [Ipswich Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ipswich_Town_F.C.) were chosen to replace them in the Football League. Gillingham returned to the Southern League.
25. The Football League Third Division North was formed in 1921, with the existing Third Division being re-designated the Third Division South.
26. There was no automatic relegation from the Football League until 1987. Gillingham thus avoided relegation despite finishing last.
27. Under a restructuring of the leagues, all members of the Southern League Division One were elevated to form the new Third Division of the expanded Football League. Gillingham thus avoided relegation despite having finished the previous season in last position.
28. Despite finishing last, New Brompton avoided relegation due to the expansion of the league.

32. New Brompton, as champions of Division Two, played the bottom team in Division One, [Swindon Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swindon_Town_F.C.), in a two-legged [test match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_match_(association_football)) and won 5–1, thus gaining promotion.
